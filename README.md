# nu-triploid

Nu-Triploid

Nutrition · Animal Nutrition · Food Safety

Nu-Triploid is a professional services initiative focused on human nutrition, animal nutrition, and food safety and hygiene.

Areas of Focus

1. Human Nutrition

- Nutrition education and dietary guidance
- Dietary pattern discussions
- Functional food and nutrition information review

2. Animal Nutrition

- Feed formulation and ration planning
- Feed ingredient and nutrient comparisons
- Farm feeding records and nutrition planning

3. Food Safety & Hygiene

- Food hazard identification
- Preventive controls and risk-reduction measures
- HACCP principles and hazard-control concepts
- Food hygiene and food-handling practices
- Food-safety documentation and compliance preparation

Approach

Nu-Triploid emphasizes practical guidance, evidence-informed information, and clear communication. Services are intended to support informed decisions in nutrition, animal feeding, and food hygiene.
